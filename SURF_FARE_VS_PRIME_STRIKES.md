# Surf Fare Feed vs Prime Strikes

## The Difference

### **Surf Fare Feed** (The Main Feed)
- **What it is**: The main list of all surf-and-flight deals
- **Shows**: All destinations with current wave height vs flight price
- **Sorted by**: Value Score (highest first)
- **Location**: The main section below Prime Strikes

### **Prime Strikes** (Special Highlighted Deals)
- **What it is**: A subset of deals that meet **exceptional criteria**
- **Criteria**: 
  - Swell Height > 3ft (0.91m) ✅
  - Swell Period > 10 seconds ✅
  - Flight Price < $500 ✅
- **Location**: Shown at the **top** of the feed with special yellow highlighting
- **Visual**: Has a pulsing "PRIME STRIKE" badge

## How They Work Together

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚡ Prime Strikes (if any exist)    │  ← Special deals at top
│  [Yellow badge, highlighted]        │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  📊 Surf-Fare Feed                  │  ← All other deals
│  [Regular deals sorted by value]    │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Example:**
- If you have 10 deals total:
  - 2 might be Prime Strikes (shown at top with badge)
  - 8 are regular deals (shown in Surf Fare Feed below)

## Why Prime Strikes Matter

Prime Strikes are the **best of the best** - they combine:
- Great surf conditions (big waves, long period)
- Cheap flights (under $500)
- High value (great surf + cheap flight = amazing deal!)

These are the deals surfers should **jump on immediately** because they're rare and valuable.
